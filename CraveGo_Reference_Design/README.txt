CraveGo — reference-inspired food delivery website

Open:
1. Extract CraveGo_Reference_Design.zip.
2. Open index.html in Google Chrome.
3. Internet connection is needed for food photos from Unsplash. All pages and cart logic run locally without a backend.

Included screens:
- Home / restaurant discovery
- Individual restaurant menu with six items per restaurant
- Separate cart page with quantities, subtotal, delivery and taxes
- Checkout form
- Order confirmation

For screenshots, open each screen and use Win + Shift + S. This is a demo project; restaurant data is illustrative and no real orders or payments are processed.
